PlusUno

Find something to do. Find someone to do it with. Connect in real life.

How might we connect young adults aged 18–25, especially those moving to a new city, with people in real life through activities, in order to build deeper connections and meaningful relationships?

User journey · tap a step to jump there

Built on our research: 6 interviews, the Mikkel persona (19, first-year student, new in the city) and the insight that the problem is not a lack of things to do, but finding what to do, who to do it with, and turning a first meeting into a lasting connection. All events and people are sample data.

How PlusUno happened

From a Miro board about youth loneliness to a clickable app prototype. A Design Sprint story in twelve panels.
